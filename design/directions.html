Aegyo: five design directions

Each direction redesigns the whole app: the home screen, the Dynamic Island and Lock Screen card she sees when you miss her, the Ring screen, the type, the motion and the sound. Everything here is animated. Tap the gem, moon or pearl to try the main interaction. Pick one, or mix parts, and I'll build it.

01

Opal Gem

Pure black, with a slowly refracting opal heart standing on a pedestal. It's quiet, premium and close to Opal: small all-caps stats and a white pill for the main action. The heart is the button.

Type

SF Pro only, tight tracking, tiny all-caps labels. Its calm comes from spacing.

Sound

A crystal singing bowl struck once, with a long, shimmering tail. The ringtone is three slow bowl tones over a soft hum.

Haptics

Lub-dub on tap. The Ring buzzes in time with the gem's heartbeat.
02

Moonlit

Moonly's night sky turned into a love letter: a bold serif headline, twinkling stars, frosted-glass cards and gold ✦ accents. The week strip is a row of moons that fill up the more you two sent love that day. You tap the moon to send love.

Type

Fraunces (bold serif) for headlines and the time, SF Pro for everything else, small all-caps labels.

Sound

A music box playing four notes over a warm pad, with a little wind-chime sparkle at the end. The ringtone is a slow lullaby phrase.

Extra

The moon strip is your real history: a full moon means you both sent love that day.
03

Aurora Hold

Northern lights drift across black glass. You press and hold the orb instead of tapping: the longer you hold, the stronger the "miss you" she receives ("held for 4 seconds"). The Ring becomes a shared breathing moment.

Type

Instrument Serif in italics for feeling-words, SF Pro for small details.

Sound

A warm analogue pad chord that swells in like a breath, with a soft felt-piano note on top. The ringtone breathes in time with the screen.

Haptics

While holding, a continuous buzz that grows stronger, then one deep thump when it sends.
04

Two Lights

Two warm points of light (you and her) joined by an arc across a grainy black screen, with the distance between you shown big like an Opal stat. Every "miss you" is a spark that travels along the arc, including inside the small Dynamic Island.

Type

SF Pro Light for big numbers, IBM Plex Mono for small labels, like an instrument panel.

Sound

Two kalimba notes, one for you and one for her, a fifth apart, with a long soft echo. The ringtone is the two notes calling back and forth.

Extra

The distance is live, using rough location you both opt into.
05

Pearl

The light-mode option. A cool pearl-white canvas with a soft pastel sheen, an iridescent pearl heart that sends out ripples like water, an elegant italic serif and black pill buttons. It's calm and gentle in the daytime. The Dynamic Island stays black, as iOS requires.

Type

DM Serif Display with italic emphasis for feelings, SF Pro for UI.

Sound

A soft harp glide that ends on a glassy bell. The ringtone is a slow, gentle harp melody.

Theme

It could switch to Direction 2 at night if you want both.
Two things to know before choosing. iOS draws the incoming-call screen itself for a real call-style Ring, so on her locked phone we control the ringtone, caller name and icon. Each Ring screen above is what she sees once the app opens, or when it's already open. The Dynamic Island always has a black background, whatever the design. For every direction, the sounds would be made at studio quality (layered, reverb, 48 kHz), not the simple tones you have now.
